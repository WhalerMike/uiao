Technical briefing, Informed Network Routing in GCC — Sections 1, 8 and 10

Decision requested

Accept the risk of exempting the Microsoft 365 Optimize endpoint set from TLS break-and-inspect at branch direct-internet egress, for the [N] pilot sites listed at Attachment A, subject to the compensating controls at clause 4 and the reversal commitments at clause 5.

Background

Microsoft 365 Informed Network Routing, which supplies Microsoft's network-quality signal to SD-WAN path selection, is not available to GCC Moderate tenants. Microsoft states this directly in its product documentation. The agency's Catalyst SD-WAN fabric will therefore steer Microsoft 365 traffic using Cloud OnRamp for SaaS probe measurements alone, with local direct internet access at branch sites.

This is a product availability limitation in the government cloud, not a FedRAMP or authorization boundary matter, and it requires no FedRAMP action.

Risk being accepted

Traffic to Microsoft 365 Optimize endpoints will egress locally at pilot branch sites without traversing the campus policy enforcement point, and will not be subject to TLS break-and-inspect or packet inspection at that egress. Content inspection of this specific traffic is forgone. Microsoft's own connectivity guidance directs that these endpoints bypass such inspection.

Scope, and why it is bounded

  • Endpoints: the Optimize category only, enumerated from the Microsoft 365 IP and URL web service, Instance=Worldwide — the instance serving GCC. The Allow and Default categories are not in scope and remain subject to existing inspection.
  • Change control: the set is machine-readable and published, with additions released 30 days before they become active. Scope is reviewed on the feed's monthly update cadence.
  • Sites: the pilot sites at Attachment A only. Fleet expansion is not authorized by this memo.
  • Architecture: classified under TIC 3.0 as Branch Office to Cloud Service Provider. Architecture review sign-off at Attachment B.

Compensating controls

  • Tenant-side content controls — [Defender for Office 365 / DLP policies in force].
  • Conditional Access enforcement on the affected services — [policy references].
  • Endpoint controls on managed devices — [EDR / configuration baseline].
  • Path and application monitoring via Cisco ThousandEyes for Government (FedRAMP Moderate authorized), with pre-cutover baselines captured at every pilot site.
  • Branch policy enforcement point telemetry reaching CISA's Cloud Log Aggregation Warehouse under reporting pattern [reference].

Reversal

Steering reverts per site within one shift by network operations, without an approval cycle. Reversion returns traffic to backhaul through the campus policy enforcement point, restoring the prior enforcement posture rather than degrading it.

Withdrawal of this exemption is a tracked step of any such rollback. The agency commits that the exemption will not remain in force at sites returned to backhaul.

Return to the Authorizing Official

The system owner will return for a further decision if: the exemption scope widens beyond the Optimize category; sites are added outside Attachment A; a compensating control named at clause 4 is found not to be in place; or the exemption is withdrawn during a rollback.
