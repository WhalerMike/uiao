**As of July 4, 2026 — Internal UIAO Working Document**

## Executive Finding

The Federal Application-Aware Networking (AAN) series (Parts 1–15, all delivered
as of July 4, 2026) provides the **evidence generation layer** for FedRAMP 20x
continuous monitoring. The Conformance Adapter / OSCAL Emitter framework provides
the **evidence governance layer** — tracking which evidence closes which control,
generating machine-readable KSI packages, and feeding the automated ConMon gate.

The OSCAL AP+AR+POAM bundle is fully generated by `uiao oscal bundle`.
The evaluation engine (ADR-111) is wired for **all 29 FedRAMP 20x CR26 KSI rules**
— zero scaffold rules remain. Part 15 (Book_15, July 4, 2026) delivered the AT
family training evidence, closed KSI-022 (KSI-CED-RAT), and activated the final
scaffold rule. All 29 KSIs are now evaluable at high confidence from slot-bound
AAN evidence. The most urgent remaining deadline is the BOD 26-04 VDR/VER
submission on **December 7, 2026**.
