## Executive Finding

The Federal Application-Aware Networking (AAN) series (Parts 1–16, all delivered
as of July 5, 2026) provides the **complete evidence and authorization layer** for
FedRAMP 20x continuous monitoring. The Conformance Adapter / OSCAL Emitter
framework provides the **evidence governance layer** — tracking which evidence
closes which control, generating machine-readable KSI packages, and feeding the
automated ConMon gate.

The OSCAL AP+AR+POAM bundle is fully generated by `uiao oscal bundle`.
The evaluation engine (ADR-111) is wired for **all 29 FedRAMP 20x CR26 KSI rules**
— zero scaffold rules remain. Part 15 (Book_15) delivered the AT family training
evidence and closed KSI-022 (KSI-CED-RAT). Part 16 (Book_16) assembled the
authorization package — OSCAL bundle (29/29 satisfied, 0 open risks, 0 POA&M
items), BOD 26-04 VDR/VER submission procedure, and CA-7 ConMon program. All 29
KSIs are satisfied at high confidence. **The series is complete and the
authorization package is ready for FedRAMP PMO submission.**
