# Federal AAN / ConMon Compliance Gap Roadmap
**As of July 4, 2026 — Internal UIAO Working Document**

---

## Executive Finding

The Federal Application-Aware Networking (AAN) series (Parts 1–14, all delivered
as of July 3, 2026) provides the **evidence generation layer** for FedRAMP 20x
continuous monitoring. The Conformance Adapter / OSCAL Emitter framework provides
the **evidence governance layer** — tracking which evidence closes which control,
generating machine-readable KSI packages, and feeding the automated ConMon gate.

The OSCAL AP+AR+POAM bundle is now fully generated by `uiao oscal bundle`.
The evaluation engine (ADR-111) is wired for **15 of 19 scaffold rules** —
four remain scaffold (KSI-015/016/020/022) pending their evidence contracts.
The most urgent remaining deadline is the BOD 26-04 VDR/VER submission on
**December 7, 2026**.
