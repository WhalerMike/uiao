Positioning · UIAO Governance Substrate

Three altitudes,
not three peers.

UIAO, OrgPath, and AAN are easy to conflate — they share a vocabulary of HR-as-truth, Zero Trust, an authoritative addressing plane, and CM-8. But they operate at different levels: UIAO is the engine, OrgPath is a plane inside it, and AAN is content that runs on top.

The stack — containment vs adjacency

On top · content vertical

AAN — Federal Application-Aware Networking

A ten-volume networking-modernization corpus + IaC/ServiceNow kits. External companion content — not part of UIAO — that maps mechanisms (IPAM/DDI, SD-WAN, TIC 3.0, 802.1X) to the NIST controls they close.

evidence ↓ consumed by adapter

UIAO — the governance substrate

The engine · vertical-agnostic core

Unified Identity-Addressing-Overlay Architecture. Turns compliance into a deterministic pipeline: one SSOT canon → drift detection → schema-enforced adapters → canon-anchored evidence. Federal compliance is its most-mature adapter pack, not the core.

Inside · the addressing plane

OrgPath  (+ LocPath)

UIAO's governed identity-addressing primitive — a derived attribute from an HR-sourced OrgTree that replaces AD's OU tree. Drives ABAC / Zero-Trust targeting and survives reorgs. LocPath is its physical-place twin.

How they relate — same words, different jobs

UIAO ⊃ OrgPath

Containment. OrgPath literally is UIAO's addressing plane — shared canon, drift taxonomy, evidence pipeline.

UIAO ← AAN

Consume & emit. A UIAO adapter (the FedRAMP AAN Evidence Catalog) binds AAN's evidence to NIST controls; the AAN concept lives in ADR-066.

OrgPath ∥ AAN

Parallel. No direct references — they connect only through shared HR-SSOT and Zero-Trust doctrine.

Value of each, standalone

UIAO

Compliance becomes a deterministic pipeline output with cryptographic provenance and drift containment — reuse and rigor across any vertical.

OrgPath

The one attribute that survives reorgs, transfers, and rejoins without manual remediation — Zero-Trust targeting that is deterministic and drift-detectable.

AAN

The federal networking playbook: which mechanisms close which controls (with necessity arguments), deployable kits, and a machine-checked spine — the evidence source UIAO attests.

Mental model: AAN builds the network substrate, OrgPath builds the identity substrate, and UIAO governs and proves both.

Grounded in the repo's own canon — ADR-085 (UIAO positioning), the OrgPath/LocPath ADR chain (035 · 048 · 088 · 098 · 102), ADR-066 (AAN concept), and the AAN corpus + its evidence adapter. The OrgPath↔AAN link is doctrinal, not yet wired at the document level.
