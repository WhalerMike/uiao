Request a DDI-backed subnet

Allocates a subnet from Infoblox IPAM, deploys it into the selected landing zone via Terraform, and registers DNS — governed by approval and validated end-to-end.
📝 Request details
Chooses the module & landing zone
Pre-populated from the landing-zone (Stage-1) outputs — not free-typed
Must fit the hub, no overlap (conflict-checked)
Drives sizing, tags & approval tier
Required only for universal_ddi. The Infoblox Portal control plane sits outside the ATO boundary; checking this triggers an additional authorization approval. Left unchecked for the in-boundary grid model.
